### Agenta

A short, broad matte-graphite channel is recessed into a warm ivory ceramic surface. Some pellets are held in an orderly bounded group just before the gate; beyond the gate the channel is clean and empty, clearly showing that excess material is not consumed. A sophisticated subtle physical metaphor for a download cutoff and bounded buffer, not a literal software diagram. Gallery-quality material photography, believable tiny glass reflections, ceramic microtexture, gentle directional sunlight, soft shadows, elegant asymmetric composition. Palette: warm ivory, charcoal graphite, amber glass and burnished copper. NO teal or cyan. Landscape 3:2 canvas; all meaningful visual detail in the upper 60 percent, with generous pale ivory negative space in the lower central third for a later HTML article title. Minimal, polished and quiet, but visually clear.
